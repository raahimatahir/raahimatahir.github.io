About Me

I am a Doctoral Candidate in Public Health and Manager of Linkages & Outreach at Ziauddin University. My work bridges institutional policy, empirical research, and sustainable development. I focus on climate resilience, anti-microbial resistance (AMR), financial inclusion, and gender-based violence prevention across vulnerable communities in South Asia.
